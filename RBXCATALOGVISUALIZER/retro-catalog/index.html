    About

    ROBLOX Catalog Visualizer is an unofficial, fan-made project.

    This site is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by or connected to Roblox Corporation in any way. ROBLOX, the ROBLOX logo and all related names, images and assets are trademarks or property of Roblox Corporation.

    It was created purely for educational purposes and as a practical tool for Roblox Studio development: browsing classic catalog items (2006–2013) with their name, ID, dates, picture and 3D preview.

    All item data is requested from public Roblox web APIs. Nothing here is sold, and no content is claimed as ours.

    The site's CSS was heavily inspired by PaperBlox PaperBlox — Roblox Papercraft Templates paperblox.com Discover, download, and build Roblox papercraft templates. Printable PDFs and step-by-step instructions. , with its layout also drawing on the classic 2008 ROBLOX website.
